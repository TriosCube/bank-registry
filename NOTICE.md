# Notice: data sources, logos and takedown

## Data
- **Nigeria** (`data/africa/NG.json`) was seeded from
  [ichtrojan/nigerian-banks](https://github.com/ichtrojan/nigerian-banks) (MIT, © 2020 Michael Okoh) and is
  maintained here from now on.
- **Other countries** were seeded from [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org) (CC0 1.0), including names, SWIFT/BIC
  codes and websites.
- **Fintechs and payment providers** in `data/fintech-seed.json` are curated by hand.

The code and data in this repository are released under the MIT License. Facts such as names and bank codes are not
copyrightable; the selection and structure are ours.

## Logos
Logos are **trademarks or copyrighted works of their respective owners**. They are included only so software can
identify an institution by its mark. Inclusion does not imply endorsement, affiliation or any licence to use the mark
beyond identifying that institution. Each logo records where it came from in the entry's `logo.source`.
Logos taken from Wikimedia Commons carry the licence shown on the linked file page.

## Takedown
If you own a logo or a record and want it changed or removed, open an issue titled `Takedown: <bank name>` or email
the maintainers. We remove the file and the `logo` field promptly, no questions asked.

## No guarantee
This is a reference list, not a regulator's register. Do not use it to decide whether an institution is licensed
or to route real payments without checking the official source (CBN, central banks, SWIFT).
